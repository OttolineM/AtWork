Who we are

Opening a door to new kinds of work.

AtWork is being built around a simple idea: everyday skills and activities can create useful, consent-based data when captured to a clear research or commercial specification.

Our aim is to create a transparent bridge between organisations that need real-world activity data and people in South Africa who want flexible ways to participate in paid projects.

Our mission

Make participation clear, fair and accountable.

  • Work only on projects with a defined brief and confirmed funding.
  • Explain recording requirements before anyone starts.
  • Review submissions against agreed quality standards.
  • Make payment terms and approval decisions transparent.
  • Respect contributor choice, consent and privacy.
For contributors

Flexible participation. Clear expectations.

AtWork intends to offer independent contributors the chance to take part in approved video-data projects from their own environments, using their own everyday skills and equipment where the project allows.

01 · CHOOSE

Choose an activity

Browse only the activities included in a real, approved project. Participation is voluntary; contributors are not required to recruit anyone.

02 · RECORD

Follow the project brief

Capture the activity as specified, including camera position, visibility, duration, landscape orientation and any project-specific equipment.

03 · REVIEW

Submit for review

Submissions are checked against the buyer's agreed requirements. A recording is not automatically accepted simply because it was uploaded.

Quality and trust

Good data starts with clear standards.

Every project should have written instructions, consent requirements, accepted formats and a review process before contributors are invited to participate.

1

Project-specific instructions

Each buyer defines the activity, video length, equipment, framing and quality criteria.

2

Contributor preparation

People receive the brief and can decide whether the task and equipment are suitable for them.

3

Human review

AtWork intends to review submissions and communicate approval or a clear reason for rejection or adjustment.

4

Transparent records

Approved time, review outcomes and payment status should be visible to contributors.

A responsible approach

Consent and privacy matter.

AtWork intends to work only with properly scoped projects and clear contributor consent. Personal information, bystanders, sensitive settings and third-party rights must be handled in line with applicable law and buyer requirements.

Data collection, storage, retention, access and transfer arrangements will be agreed before any project begins.

How the model is intended to work

Buyer-funded projects, transparent contributor terms.

A buyer defines and funds a project. AtWork coordinates contributor participation and quality review. Accepted data is delivered under agreed terms, and contributor payments are made according to the funded project agreement.

AtWork aims to avoid charging workers to join or requiring them to recruit others. The platform's operating revenue would come from commercial project agreements, not recruitment fees.

Our commitment

Clear terms before any work begins

Before a project opens, contributors will be told what the activity involves, how recordings are reviewed, what qualifies for approval, how much the project pays and when payment is due.

Rates and project arrangements will depend on the buyer's requirements and confirmed funding. No earnings are being promised at this stage.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I sign up and start earning today?

Not yet. AtWork is still being developed and is validating buyer demand and funding. No paid tasks are currently being offered through this website.

Do contributors pay a joining fee?

No joining or recruitment fee is part of AtWork's intended model. Be cautious of anyone claiming to represent AtWork who asks you to pay to access work.

Do I have to recruit other people?

No. Contributors are not expected to recruit others. Participation is based on the project requirements and the contributor's own choice.

Will every activity listed here be paid?

No. Activities become available only when a buyer has approved the activity brief and sufficient project funding and terms are in place.

Where do recordings take place?

The intended model is independent recording in a suitable environment, often at home. AtWork is not a service that sends contributors to customers' homes.

How will payment work?

Payment amounts, accepted minutes, review standards and payment timing will be disclosed in each funded project's terms before participation begins.

Who reviews the videos?

AtWork intends to use a documented review process, with human oversight and clear reasons when a submission does not meet the agreed brief.
